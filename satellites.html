🛰️ SATELLITES

What is a Satellite?

A satellite is an object that moves around a planet or another object in space. Artificial satellites are designed and launched by humans for different purposes.

Uses of Satellites

• Communication

• Weather forecasting

• GPS and navigation

• Earth observation

• Scientific research

How Satellites Help Us

Satellites help us communicate across long distances, predict weather, study Earth and navigate from one location to another.

Future of Satellites

Future satellites will provide better communication, improved Earth observation and advanced scientific information.
